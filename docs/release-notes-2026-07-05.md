# What's New — July 5, 2026

A summary of recent improvements to Payroll — Cash Advances.

---

## Payroll — Cash Advances

### Employees can now have more than one active cash advance
- Requesting a new cash advance is no longer blocked just because the employee already has one unpaid. Multiple cash advances can now be active for the same employee at the same time.
- When generating a payroll period, deductions are applied across **all** of an employee's active cash advances — oldest one first — until the period's pay is used up or every advance is paid off.

### Deleting or voiding a payroll period now reverses cash advance deductions
- Previously, deleting a draft payroll period (or voiding an approved one) left any cash advance balance reduced — or even marked fully paid — with no way to get that money back onto the employee's ledger.
- Both actions now restore the exact amount that period took from each cash advance, reopening it if it had been marked paid off. If a cash advance has been touched by more than one period, deleting or voiding one of them only undoes that period's share — any other period's effect on the same advance is left alone.
